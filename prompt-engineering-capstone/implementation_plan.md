## Phase Deliverables

| Phase                                  | Duration   | Primary Deliverables                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **1. Planning & Data Cleanup**         | Month 1    | Requirements baseline, cleaned CRM dataset, data mapping, migration plan                   |
| **2. System Configuration & Pilot**    | Months 2–3 | Configured HubSpot environment, workflows/integrations, pilot results, UAT sign-off        |
| **3. Full Migration & Staff Training** | Months 4–5 | Production data migration, data validation, training completion, organization-wide go-live |
| **4. Post-Launch Optimization**        | Month 6    | Adoption metrics, issue resolution, workflow optimization, improvement backlog             |

### Key Stage Gates

**End of Month 1 →** Clean data and approved migration design
**End of Month 3 →** Pilot/UAT approval
**End of Month 5 →** Full migration validated and 150 users trained
**End of Month 6 →** Optimization review and transition to business-as-usual operations

**Project outcome:** A controlled six-month transition from the existing CRM environment to HubSpot, with configuration and data quality validated before organization-wide adoption.

| Risk Event                                                  | Probability (Low/Med/High) | Impact (Low/Med/High) | Mitigation                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Poor data quality or duplicate records during migration** | High                       | High                  | Conduct data profiling and cleansing in Month 1; establish deduplication rules, field mapping, validation checks, and a backup before migration.                                  |
| **User resistance or low adoption among 150 sales reps**    | Med                        | High                  | Use pilot users, role-based training, hands-on workshops, clear adoption targets, and post-go-live support. Identify CRM champions within the sales team.                         |
| **Configuration/integration issues delay go-live**          | Med                        | High                  | Define requirements upfront, configure in a sandbox/test environment, conduct integration testing and UAT, maintain a prioritized issue log, and require formal go-live sign-off. |
