# Slide 1: Title & Executive Overview

## Apex Cloud Services — CRM Vendor Selection

**Objective:** Select and implement a CRM platform for **150 sales representatives**.

### Executive Takeaways

* Three vendors evaluated: **Salesforce, HubSpot, and Zoho**.
* Three-year TCO ranges from **$221K to $835K**.
* Weighted decision model assessed **cost, customization & scale, setup speed, and user adoption**.
* **Board-selected solution: HubSpot CRM**.
* Planned implementation: **6 months across four phases**.

**Strategic objective:** Establish a scalable CRM foundation while controlling implementation cost and accelerating sales-team adoption.

---

# Slide 2: Client Problem & Current Metrics

## CRM Transformation Requirement

### Business Context

* **150 sales representatives** require migration to a centralized CRM.
* Organization needs a platform capable of supporting sales processes, customer data, workflows, and future growth.
* Migration requires controlled data cleansing, configuration, user training, and post-launch optimization.

### Financial Baseline

| Metric                |                Value |
| --------------------- | -------------------: |
| Sales users           |              **150** |
| Evaluation horizon    | **1 year / 3 years** |
| Salesforce 3-Year TCO |            **$835K** |
| HubSpot 3-Year TCO    |            **$496K** |
| Zoho 3-Year TCO       |            **$221K** |

**Key issue:** Balance total ownership cost against implementation speed, scalability, customization, and user adoption.

# Slide 4: Cost-Benefit Analysis & TCO

## Three-Year Economics

### TCO Calculation

**Salesforce**

`150 × $150 × 36 + $25,000 = $835,000`

**HubSpot**

`150 × $90 × 36 + $10,000 = $496,000`

**Zoho**

`150 × $40 × 36 + $5,000 = $221,000`

### Financial Comparison

| Vendor     | 1-Year TCO | 3-Year TCO | 3-Year Recurring + Setup |
| ---------- | ---------: | ---------: | -----------------------: |
| Salesforce |      $295K |  **$835K** |             $810K + $25K |
| HubSpot    |      $172K |  **$496K** |             $486K + $10K |
| Zoho       |       $77K |  **$221K** |              $216K + $5K |

**Financial observation:** Zoho has the lowest TCO, while HubSpot sits between Zoho and Salesforce on cost.

# Slide 5: Decision Matrix & Recommendation

## Weighted Evaluation

| Criteria              |   Weight | Salesforce |  HubSpot |     Zoho |
| --------------------- | -------: | ---------: | -------: | -------: |
| TCO Cost Model        |      30% |          4 |        7 |       10 |
| Customization & Scale |      20% |         10 |        8 |        8 |
| Setup Speed           |      25% |          6 |        9 |        8 |
| User Adoption Ease    |      25% |          7 |        9 |        8 |
| **Weighted Total**    | **100%** |   **6.45** | **8.20** | **8.60** |

### Board Decision

**HubSpot CRM was selected by the board as the solution to implement.**

### Decision Rationale

* Strong balance across **implementation speed and user adoption**.
* Lower TCO than Salesforce under the supplied pricing assumptions.
* Provides a middle-ground option between the lowest-cost platform and the higher-cost enterprise platform.
* The selection reflects the board's decision to balance financial and operational considerations rather than TCO alone.

**Important:** The weighted scores are an analytical decision model based on the stated assumptions, not independently validated vendor ratings.

# Slide 6: Phased Roadmap & Immediate Next Steps

## Six-Month Implementation Roadmap

| Phase                          | Timeline       | Core Activities                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Planning & Data Cleanup** | **Month 1**    | Requirements, data audit, cleansing, deduplication, migration mapping |
| **2. Configuration & Pilot**   | **Months 2–3** | CRM configuration, workflows, integrations, pilot, UAT                |
| **3. Migration & Training**    | **Months 4–5** | Production migration, validation, staff training, go-live             |
| **4. Optimization**            | **Month 6**    | Adoption monitoring, issue resolution, workflow optimization          |

### Immediate Next Steps

1. **Establish project governance** and assign business/technical owners.
2. **Finalize requirements and data-migration rules**.
3. **Begin Month 1 data audit and cleansing.**
4. Establish **pilot group and UAT criteria**.
5. Define **go-live readiness and adoption KPIs**.
6. Maintain a risk register covering **data quality, user adoption, and configuration/integration issues**.

### Success Measure

**Six-month outcome:** A validated HubSpot CRM deployment for **150 sales representatives**, with migrated data, trained users, controlled go-live, and a defined optimization cycle.
