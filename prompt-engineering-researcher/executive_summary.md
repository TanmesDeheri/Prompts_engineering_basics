**Based on the finalized comparison, Kubernetes, Docker Swarm, and AWS Fargate represent different approaches to container deployment. Kubernetes offers high scalability and extensive orchestration capabilities, but it also introduces higher setup complexity and operational overhead. Docker Swarm provides a simpler Docker-integrated orchestration model with moderate scalability and operational requirements. AWS Fargate abstracts the underlying compute infrastructure, reducing host-management responsibilities while providing task-level resource allocation and consumption-based pricing.**

**For a fast-growing startup prioritizing speed over infrastructure scaling, AWS Fargate is the most suitable choice among the three. Its managed compute model allows the team to deploy containers without provisioning or maintaining worker-node infrastructure, reducing operational effort and accelerating deployment. The startup still needs to configure ECS or EKS, IAM, networking, security, monitoring, and application resources, so Fargate does not eliminate all operational work.**
